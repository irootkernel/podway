`8a1f2ad4727617239cae4cd809bcedf215c93c07` and is available from the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/irootkernel/podway/releases/tag/v0.1.1).

## Published outcome

The release published the native Apple Silicon archive, detached checksum,
provenance, and Dolgorae compatibility handoff. The historical task statuses in
the active roadmap were not advanced before publication; this archive reconciles
them with the published outcome. The v0.1.2 work does not modify or supersede the
v0.1.1 tag or asset bytes.
